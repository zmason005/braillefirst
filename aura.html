AURA

Flagship project — active development

What AURA does

AURA is an accessible AI orchestration interface that connects blind professionals directly to generative AI platforms and enterprise developer environments. It renders every response as stable, structured text: consistent heading levels, predictable reading order, and no layout shifts that break a screen reader's place.

Live regions announce updates without interrupting input, and results are grouped so a braille display shows related information on a single line rather than splitting it across cells. AURA sits between a person and their tools — version control, cloud consoles, project trackers — turning verbose interfaces into something a Braille reader can navigate and direct with confidence, not just consume passively.

Status

AURA is under active development. A live demo and source access will be linked here as they become available. In the meantime, use the contact form on the homepage to ask questions or get updates.
